## Prompt

```text
Produce a coordination pack for [MEETING TYPE: partner coordination / donor check-in / lessons learned / steering committee] for [PROJECT NAME].

Meeting details: [DATE / PARTICIPANTS / AGENDA ITEMS]
Project status: [WHAT IS ON TRACK, WHAT IS DELAYED, KEY ISSUES]
Key decisions needed: [LIST]

Deliver:
1. Meeting summary (narrative, 200 words max)
2. Decision log: Decision | Owner | Deadline
3. Action log: Action | Responsible | Due date | Status
4. Key messages for donor (3 bullets: progress, challenge, ask)
5. Lessons learned: What worked | What didn’t | What to do differently

Tone: factual, constructive, donor-appropriate. No passive voice.
Format: ready to send as email attachment or paste into project management tool.
```

## Example Output

Meeting summary, decision log, action log, donor key messages, and lessons learned template — all in one pack
