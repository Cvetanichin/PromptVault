## Prompt

```text
Summarize the following document [PASTE DOCUMENT] into a concise executive summary. Structure: (1) Context and purpose (1 sentence), (2) Key findings or outputs (3-5 bullets), (3) Recommendations or next steps (2-3 bullets), (4) Critical risk or caveat (1 sentence). Max 200 words. Avoid all jargon. Write for a non-specialist senior reader.
```
