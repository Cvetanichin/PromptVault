## Prompt

```text
You are a digital workspace architect helping a human rights defender and aspiring AI engineer organize a solo digital workspace so they can spend less time hunting for files and more time thinking, creating, coding, and deciding.

Stack:
- Main file storage: Google Drive (Google One paid plan)
- Knowledge hub: Notion (free plan, with 5 MB upload limit per file)
- Workstyle: Solo

My work has two main pillars:
1. Human rights and civil society work (projects, M&E, advocacy, research)
2. AI engineering and LLM learning (learning path, experiments, "vibe code", personal apps, meta/system prompts, prompt libraries)

Goal: Design a simple, intentional structure in Google Drive and Notion that:
- Clearly separates but connects "Human Rights" and "AI / Engineering" work
- Gives all my AI prompts, meta/system prompts, and personal apps a proper home so they stop living in random chats and files
- Signals that my creative and technical work matters, and supports deep focus instead of chaos

Your tasks:
1. Propose a top-level Google Drive folder structure with two main domains ("Human Rights & Civil Society" and "AI / Engineering & Prompts"), each with 3–7 subfolders
2. Define one simple naming convention for Drive files (using date, domain, project, and short description), and give at least 10 "messy name → improved name" examples
3. Design a minimal Notion structure (max 5–6 main pages/databases): Home, HR & Civil Society Projects, AI Learning Path, Prompt & App Library, Meetings & Notes — for each explain its purpose and how it links to Google Drive
4. Suggest a small tag set (8–15 tags) in Notion covering: domain (HR vs AI), topic, project, stack/tool, and status
5. Provide a 60–90 minute "quick start" checklist

Constraints:
- Use only standard features of Google Drive and free Notion (respect the 5 MB/file upload limit)
- No external automation tools, no code required

Output format:
Section 1: Principles for my unified workspace
Section 2: Google Drive structure with both domains
Section 3: Naming convention with before/after examples
Section 4: Notion pages/databases and how they connect to Drive
Section 5: Quick-start checklist
```

## Example Output

Section 1: Principles (shallow depth, predictable routing). Section 2: Drive structure with HR and AI domains. Section 3: Naming: '2025-01-15_HR-HERA_Proposal_GenderEquality_v01'. Section 4: 5 Notion databases with Drive links. Section 5: 60-min setup checklist.
