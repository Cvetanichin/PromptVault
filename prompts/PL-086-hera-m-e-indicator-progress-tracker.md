## Prompt

```text
Act as a senior M&E specialist for an EU-funded project. Review the indicator data for [PROJECT NAME] and produce a quarterly progress tracker. Inputs: logframe indicators, baseline, target, current value, reporting period, evidence source, and implementation notes. For each indicator, calculate progress against target, classify status as On Track / Watch / Off Track, flag data-quality issues, and recommend one corrective action. Output a donor-ready table plus a short management summary.
```

## Example Output

Indicator tracker table with baseline, target, actual, progress %, status, evidence gap, and corrective action, followed by a 150-word summary.
